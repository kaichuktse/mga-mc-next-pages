Two pieces of work are paced by outside parties and don't shorten in any delivery model: carrier approval of the SMS short codes (12 weeks, needed before the triggered flows start) and email domain warm-up with mailbox providers (6 weeks). Carrier approval holds the flows phase at week 13 in every model. Warm-up starts with the Klaviyo consent import at the end of Phase 2, so it no longer sets the length of the flows phase.

AI-native is conditional. It holds only if MGA commits to the way of working: approved AI tooling, a named product owner who decides quickly, sandbox access, and a privacy position on AI-assisted delivery with children's data. None is confirmed today.

Efficiency bands: AI-augmented ~10–21% (Mid readiness); AI-native ~16–34% (conditional, provisional).
