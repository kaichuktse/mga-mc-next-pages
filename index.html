Overview · reduced scope

Three Klaviyo accounts become one Marketing Cloud Next org.

MGA runs three Klaviyo accounts today, one per Shopify storefront (MGA Shop, Bratz and Little Tikes), with no shared customer view and separate opt-ins. This program moves all three onto one Marketing Cloud Next org on one Data 360 data space, so MGA can recognize one parent across the three stores and its Snapser apps, with one opt-in across the brand umbrella. In this reduced scope, Salesforce builds 8 of the 39 Klaviyo flows and loads every data source; MGA builds, tests and switches over the other 31 flows, with enablement from Salesforce. MGA's target is March 1, 2027; Klaviyo can run month to month if launch moves past it.

Two pieces of work are paced by outside parties and don't shorten in any delivery model: carrier approval of the SMS short codes (12 weeks, needed before the triggered flows start) and email domain warm-up with mailbox providers (6 weeks). They set a 22-week floor for both AI-assisted models, so the AI-native advantage is team size, not calendar.

AI-native is conditional. It holds only if MGA commits to the way of working: approved AI tooling, a named product owner who decides quickly, sandbox access, and a privacy position on AI-assisted delivery with children's data. None is confirmed today.
